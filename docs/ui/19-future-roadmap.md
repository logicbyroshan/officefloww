# 19 — Future Product Roadmap

The architectural foundation established in Phase 3 supports continuous evolution for Adharsh Bhopal OS:

---

## 1. Near-Term Milestones

1. **Embedded SQLite Offline Replica**:
   - Synchronize local read-only replicas of clients and products using SQLite WASM in Electron, guaranteeing instant offline performance.
2. **Camera-Based AI Optical Quality Inspection**:
   - Direct webcam / industrial camera feed into the QC stage to automatically flag misaligned lanyard logos or heat transfer defects.
3. **Automated WhatsApp / SMS Notification Gateway**:
   - Instant client proof approval requests sent to school coordinators via WhatsApp template messages directly from `Clients`.
4. **Machine Predictive Maintenance Alerts**:
   - Monitor temperature logs and cycle hours on rotary sublimation presses to trigger automated oiling and maintenance tickets.
